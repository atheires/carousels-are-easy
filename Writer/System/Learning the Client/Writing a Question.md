# Writing a Question

*The craft rules for step 2. This is the part that goes wrong.*

---

**Say the subject. Never encode it.** This is the single most common failure. *"How do I find clients who actually have a budget?"* is a dressed-up way of saying **"How do I find good clients?"** The dressed-up version reads as insight and is worse, because the reader has to decode it to find out whether it's their question. If the real subject is saturation, the question says *saturated*. If it's cold outreach, it says *cold outreach*.

**Short.** Most real questions are five to ten words. If it's running past twelve, something is being hidden in a clause.

**No cleverness, no hindsight framing, no sugarcoating.** *"Why does everything dry up the moment I stop hustling?"* is a writer performing. *"Why do my clients never come back?"* is a person asking. Write the second one.

**Their words, typed at speed.** The test: would they type this into Google, post it on Reddit, or send it in a DM? If it only works as a headline, it's not a question — it's a topic dressed as one. Topics get made in step 3.

**Fewer real questions beat more padded ones.** Fifty clear questions are worth more than a hundred where sixty are rephrasings. Never generate to hit a count.

**Keep awareness levels clean inside a block.** A scaling question does not belong in a getting-started section — the person asking one isn't the person asking the other.

**Harvest three kinds, not one.** A file of only pains is a file that can only produce complaint posts:
- **Concerns** — what's going wrong. The bulk of it.
- **Dream-state** — *"What does a good client actually look like?" "What does a day at $10k a month look like?"* What the good version looks like, asked sincerely.
- **Limiting beliefs** — *"Who am I to charge for this?" "Am I upskilling or am I procrastinating?"* The thing they think and don't say. Highest resonance material in the file.

**Ground it in real language.** Search Reddit and Quora for how this audience actually phrases things before writing. Copy the phrasing; don't improve it.

**Write everything down. Bad ones included.** A question you'd reject on instinct still passes through step 3, where it gets scored or moved down a rung. Rejecting at harvest time is how a file ends up with twelve safe topics in it — but "don't reject" is not permission to pad.

Group by sub-dream, tag each with its awareness level, and mark the dream-state and limiting-belief ones.

Save to `Clients/[name]/Burning Questions.md`. Show it to the client before continuing — this is the file they'll have the most opinions about, and their reaction is worth more than another twenty questions.

**The bank is permanent and it grows.** Every session, questions get added and used ones get marked. It is never rebuilt from scratch.
