## 5. Type system

Punch: very large bold italic editorial serif on cover and CTA keyword. Title: bold upright serif on selected body openings. Connector: white loose handwritten text over the photo, often uppercase in the source. Body: black, rounded monoline handwriting, comfortable spacing and natural paragraph blocks. Label: handwritten text on pale aqua highlight or above CTA keyword. Handle: no consistent brand handle furniture; evidence screenshot watermarks are part of the source screenshot, not a brand element to reproduce.

Do not assert exact font names. Match stroke contrast, slant, rounded handwriting and hierarchy. Current copy capitalization takes precedence over reference capitalization.

## 6. Color and texture system

Warm cream paper, near-black ink, white tape/doodle/bridges, textured antique-gold stars. Pale aqua/teal only at major cover/CTA emphasis. Photographs supply broad environmental color: green foliage, warm neutrals, clothing color and blue water. Red enters selected body X lists and yellow sparkles enter the leisure list; neither is a required global accent. Paper/tape/grain supply tactility without overwhelming the casual photo.

## 7. Continuity mechanics

Same narrator, cream-paper geometry, handwriting, tape, gold stars and white plane recur even when settings change. Emotional pacing comes from tighter indoor frames at difficulty, ordinary activities during action, open outdoor environments at relief, and the café returning at the end. These relationships are interpretations of the source, not rigid rules that every positive sentence needs an outdoor image.

Cover: restrained italic serif hook with one highlighted word/phrase and no subtitle by default. The white cutout contour recurs whenever the subject is foregrounded, including body slides. Body: handwriting dominance, flexible note placement, frequent meaningful white photo text, and occasional serif headings. CTA: a large aqua-highlighted keyword; return to the cover scene only when it intentionally closes the narrative.

Type production: generate finished typography and inspect every character per the current workflow; no requirement for manual typography or user reconstruction. Font sizes, safe areas and per-slide scene choices are specified in Carousel 14/Branding.md.

Checklist: background serves slide meaning; consistent user likeness; photo looks casual; face/gesture unobstructed; legible paper text; exact words; no false evidence; restrained motifs; repeated tip template; distinct cover/CTA roles; 3:4.

Weaknesses to improve: stars sometimes clutter edges and overlap important subjects; paper can cover too much of the image; white bridge text sometimes competes with busy foliage; dense handwriting and long lines can slow reading. Keep the emotional logic while tightening these execution issues. Do not adopt earnings claims or promotional promises from source text.
