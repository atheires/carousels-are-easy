# Hair cutout cover with simple typography body

Source: four user-supplied images, 2026-09-20. Two cover and two body examples; unseen slides are not inferred. Inspiration, not instructions or a source of verified medical claims.

## 1. Build method
Hybrid photographic cutouts and flat typography. The original production method is unknown. Our workflow generates finished type and visuals together.

## 2. Cover construction
Large upper centered bold sans headline, white italic brush emphasis, isolated hair photographs below. One cover uses two hair views and a drawn arrow; the other one large split hair view. Borrow clarity and scale, not the exact transformation composition.

## 3. Body slide format
Spacious flat background, bold heading, two short explanatory blocks or a compact checklist, then one large scissors icon. Optional handwritten emphasis line. One topic per slide.

## 4. Body visual treatment
Body drops photography for typography and one simple pictogram. No cards, complex diagrams or photographic scenes. Scissors act as a semantic anchor.

## 5. Type system
Punch/title: heavy rounded sans. Cover emphasis: white brush italic. Body: readable medium/bold sans. Occasional brush annotation. Original watermark and carousel navigation are not brand elements to reproduce.

## 6. Color and texture
Two observed background families: muted taupe and pale lavender. Brown or dark purple headings, black body text, white accent. User permits entirely new paired background colors. Sparse starbursts on covers; body backgrounds are quieter.

## 7. Continuity
Flat paired backgrounds, heading weight, simple isolated visual, consistent text contrast. New account direction should repeat the chosen pair across alternating slides.

## 8. Reproduction kit
Cover: 3:4, one direct hook, high contrast type, a single original subject tied to hair care, ample safe margins. Body: chosen flat background, one readable heading when helpful, short plain sans copy, selective emphasis, one meaningful icon or image. Generate typography with the image tool. Check spelling, thumbnail legibility, margins, and hierarchy. Avoid cloning exact layout, sparkle scatter, UI buttons, watermark or fabricated before/after results.

Weaknesses: original taupe/brown heading contrast is subdued; repeated decorative sparkles can dilute focus. New proposals emphasize stronger color contrast and distinct compositions.
