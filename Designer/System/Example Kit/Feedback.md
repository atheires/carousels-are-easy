# Feedback

Every line is a standing rule for this branding.

## 10th October 2026, first cover ("nah not good")

- **No legs.** Mascots rise from the very bottom edge of the page, cropped at the waist or chest like the people in the reference. Never show feet or full bodies standing on the page.
- **Every mascot a different colour.** Each one gets its own outfit and hat colour, like the reference cast.
- **Cover cast is five, in two rows.** The two biggest at the front (ChatGPT's mascot and Claude's mascot), then behind them the handler in the middle with the writer and the designer on either side.
- **Better shading.** The first mascots felt flat. Give them soft dimensional shading, highlights and depth.
- **Clear gap between the text and the characters.** The arrowed labels live in that gap, so they never crowd the headline or subtitle.
- **Realistic shadows.** Soft natural cast shadows, never hard geometric diagonal blocks.

## 10th October 2026, second cover ("yes better")

- **Mascots a bit shorter on the cover.** Leave more empty space between the subheading and the arrowed labels.
- **Five characters is the rule.** Every cover keeps a cast of five with clothes and different personalities. They can be the mascots, objects turned into mascots, or animal-like characters, chosen from the text and the idea of that carousel.
- **More is welcome.** Extra small mascots can be added and faded into the background so the slide feels like a fun poster, as long as the main five stay.
- **Body slides and the CTA carry the mascots too**, adding to the scene. The CTA slide should look good in its own right.
- The soft 3D look of the second cover was accepted ("yes better").

Standing rules:

- **An imaginary border on all four sides.** Text never crosses it: top, left, right, and the bottom line where the mascots begin. He said "maybe 20 pixels"; keep it the same on every slide.
- **Leave negative space.** Never write on every pixel. Let the text breathe.
- **Main text big, never huge.** If the heading stops the eye reading anything else, it is too big.
- **Proper spacing** between the heading and the body, and between paragraphs.
- **A better body font.** The first one was rejected.
- **Break long text into bullets or short chunks.** Change the presentation, never the copy.
- **Numbering is plain:** "1. The Swipe File". No giant outlined numerals.
- **Sets of numbers sit in one row**, small enough to leave space around them.
- **Mascots never take half the slide.** On body slides they are small.
- **Mascots must have a purpose.** They go with the concept of the post, add to what the slide is saying and belong in the scene. If they add nothing, shrink them or leave them out.
- **No colour change between slides.** The background stays the same.
- **Concepts worth keeping:** the notebook page (7), the speech or thought bubble (5), the pull quote (2).
