# Copy check with the 10 laws

Stage 1 review protocol. Writing and rewriting are authorized as of 2026-09-12;
follow `System/Carousel writing workflow.md` and its complete playbook first.
The ten laws below are supporting packaging checks. Where they conflict with the
playbook's content-led structure, use the playbook and logged user preferences.

## Current design-intake behavior

Follow `Master workflow.md`. Copy and references arrive together. Run routine checks internally and keep the full scorecard for explicit review requests. Briefly flag material concerns without imposing a routine copy-approval gate. Preserve supplied copy unless revision is requested; explicit user approval overrides subjective packaging flags. Never claim failed checks passed. Use `Carousel Cheatsheet.md` as the actual cheatsheet filename.

## How to run the check

1. Read `Memory/Copy feedback log.md` first. Honor every entry.
2. Read the user's full carousel copy. Identify what will be the COVER hook (headline +
   subline) — the laws apply most heavily there.
3. Score the copy against each applicable law below. For each: PASS or FAIL with a one-line
   reason. For every FAIL, explain specifically what's wrong and what direction fixes it
   (use the hook-rewrite formulas in Part 4 of the cheatsheet as pointers, and
   provide replacement copy only when writing or revision is requested).

## The 10 laws (copy-relevant reading)

1. **Outcome, emotion, or secret — never description.** The hook must promise a payoff,
   a feeling, or insider knowledge. If it describes the content/photo, FAIL.
2. **Two-tier title.** Copy must split into ONE huge phrase + ONE small subline. A single
   flat line is a FAIL. Check the copy actually has both tiers.
3. **(Design law — font.)** Not a copy check, skip unless copy dictates styling.
4. **(Design law — framing.)** Not a copy check.
5. **(Design law — supporting elements.)** Copy check angle: does the copy give material
   for labels/annotations/supporting text? Flag if there's nothing to work with.
6. **(Design law — contrast.)** Not a copy check.
7. **(Design law — cutout.)** Not a copy check.
8. **(Design law — borrowed format.)** Copy check angle: does the copy naturally suggest a
   known format (before/after, POV, rules-of, grid tease)? Note the best match.
9. **Emotion / storytelling over facts.** If the copy is a bare fact or listicle with no
   emotional charge, identity claim, or story, FAIL.
10. **Bold-able keyword in the subline.** There must be one or two words in the subline
    worth bolding — the emotional punch or surprise word. If everything is flat, FAIL.

Also run the copy through:
- **Part 5 Skip checklist** (red flags) — flag applicable concerns; user approval and the current intake workflow govern progression.
- **Punchword scan** — does the hook carry a loaded word (viral, unwritten, toxic, ruined,
  altered, magic, POV, ultimate, secret, lies, art of, rules of, anatomy of...)? If not,
  flag it, but this is a suggestion, not an automatic fail.

## Output format when a detailed review is requested

```
COPY CHECK — [carousel name]

Law 1 (Promise not description): PASS/FAIL — reason
Law 2 (Two-tier title): PASS/FAIL — reason
Law 9 (Emotion over facts): PASS/FAIL — reason
Law 10 (Bold-able keyword): PASS/FAIL — reason
Supporting material (Law 5 angle): OK/FLAG — reason
Format match (Law 8 angle): best-fit format, e.g. "rules-of / listicle-secret"
Punchword: present/missing — suggestion direction

Verdict: PASSES / NEEDS REDO
Fix directions: [numbered, specific, one per failure]
```

Save the supplied copy with its true status in `Copy.md`. Continue directly to cover generation when references or a chosen brand are available. Otherwise present the workspace branding library and ask which to reuse. Do not ask for cover inspiration as a mandatory separate step.
