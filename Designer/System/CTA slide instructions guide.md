# Closing slide generation guide

Generate the final slide within the automatic post-cover batch. Follow `Master workflow.md`; no separate approval is needed.

Read current `Branding.md`, `Copy.md`, and `Memory/CTA slide feedback log.md`. Use the supplied closing copy verbatim. If it is motivational rather than a CTA, design it as supplied. Do not invent a keyword, offer, follow request, reward, or delivery promise.


Prepare the brief internally. Give the ending a clear focal point, readable action/reward where present, and continuity with the cover. A deliberate change in density may signal the close.

Generate, visually inspect, fix defects and save as the final numbered PNG. Verify and package the entire set. Record permanent feedback in the CTA log. Show instructions only on request.
