**the ship of theseus**

if you replace every part of something over time, is it still the same thing? basically, what actually makes something "the same"?

**3 — [tuesday]**

**the veil of ignorance**

imagine creating the rules of society without knowing who you'd be in it. you'd probably make rules that are fair to everyone.

*Black-and-white photo of a blindfolded man.*

**4 — [wednesday]**

**nihilism**

there is no built-in meaning to life. meaning isn't something you find—it may be something you create.

**5 — [thursday]**

**the is-ought problem**

just because something is a certain way doesn't mean it should be that way. facts alone don't tell us what we ought to do.

**6 — [friday]**

**confirmation bias**

we naturally look for information that proves us right and ignore information that challenges us.

*Painting of a hooded figure.*

**7 — [saturday]**

**utilitarianism**

the best decision is the one that creates the most overall happiness and the least suffering.

*Illustration of a hand tipping a coin.*

**the map is not the territory**

our ideas and models about reality aren't reality itself. don't confuse the way you understand something with the thing itself.

## Notes

**The cover is a part two.** "pt. 2" tells the reader this series already worked, and the verb "nerd out on" sets the tone: this is for fun, not for study. The mirror selfie keeps it a person's list.

**Every card is the same build.** A weekday in brackets, a big black title on a white label across a strange picture, and two short sentences in lower case. The explanation is one idea in plain words ("meaning isn't something you find—it may be something you create"), so a reader with no philosophy background can take it in a few seconds.

**The weekday tags give the list a calendar.** Seven concepts for seven days implies the reader is meant to take one a day, which turns a list into a habit. The saved file order mixes Friday and Saturday, so the tags are the only order.

**The CTA offers video links.** "Comment 'philo' to get video links to all of them" is a free next step with a very small ask, and the comment word is the topic shortened. Compare [[02 - How to get ridiculously better at art]] for another list that points to outside material.
