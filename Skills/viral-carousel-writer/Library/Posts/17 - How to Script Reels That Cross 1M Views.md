## Notes

**Time-stamped structure instead of numbered steps.** *Seconds 3-20 / Seconds 21-30 / Last 2 seconds.* Because the medium is time-based, the framework is expressed in the reader's own unit. The carousel equivalent would be slide ranges — which is exactly what post 15 does (*"Slide 3–8: Value that builds momentum"*).

**"Your audience remembers the feeling, not the reel."** The best line in the post, and it generalises past reels. It is the argument for why the payoff has to be *satisfying* rather than *impressive* — and it explains why post 11 (the two cakes) works with no information in it at all.

**"The payoff doesn't need to be crazy. It needs to be satisfying. It needs to answer the question."** Three short sentences that lower the bar and raise the standard at the same time. Useful against the temptation to make every ending a revelation.

**The workflow slide keeps the human in the loop, visibly.** *"Claude generates hook variations → You choose best"*, *"Claude identifies weak moments → You restructure"*, *"Claude generates payoff options → You pick"*. Three of six steps hand the decision back. This is the same division of labour as *you are the writer, the client is the author* — and it is worth noting it is presented as a **selling point**, not a limitation.

**"This takes 30 minutes. Without Claude, it takes 3 hours of guessing."** Two numbers and one word doing the work. *Guessing* is the real claim — the saving is not time, it is certainty.

**Each teaching slide is: eyebrow label → time range → the question to ask → what it does → a bolded consequence line.** Five identical beats, three times. The bolded last line is always the cost of getting it wrong (*"Mid-roll retention is where most reels fail"*, *"feel like wasted time"*, *"leaves engagement on the table"*). **Ending each slide on the penalty rather than the benefit.**

**Third weak CTA from this account.** *"Comment 🔥 if you want more reel scripting frameworks like this."* Consistent pattern: strong teaching, no deliverable, no capture.
