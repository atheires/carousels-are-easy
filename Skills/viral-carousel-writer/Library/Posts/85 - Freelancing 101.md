## Notes

**The design is a notes app.** Every slide carries a timestamp and a "Work" tag, as if the reader is looking at someone's private checklist. The format signals "no polish, just the steps", which suits a beginner post and lets the text run longer than a designed slide would.

**The cover gives a length, "8 weeks", that the post then changes.** The cover says 8 weeks, slide 6 titles the bonus "6-week roadmap" and runs to week 8. The mismatch is in the original.

**The path is four slides of plain steps.** Pick an interest, learn the skills, build a portfolio, follow a roadmap. Each step gives the exact action ("Search '[your skill] tutorial'", "Export as PDF", "Save in Google Drive (set to 'Anyone can view')"), so a beginner can do it in the next ten minutes.

**The sources are other creators' videos.** Slides 7 and 8 recommend four YouTube videos with thumbnails and credit each creator. The author is curating, not claiming to know everything, which makes the free mini course on the last slide read as the next step and not as the pitch.

**The CTA names the author's own pick.** "I chose freelance social media management and if you are also interested in the same..." narrows the offer to one path, and the comment word "SMM MINICOURSE" matches. Compare [[64 - Your First $1,000 as a Freelancer in the Next 30 Days]] for the same audience.
