## Notes

**The cover leads with hours saved and puts a parent in the frame.** "10+ hours" is the benefit, and the toddler photo says this person has no spare time, so the saving is believable. The second slide names the belief to drop: "I don't use Claude just to write content. I use it as my content system."

**Five steps, each one a thing to open or type.** Create the project, train it on your voice, create skills, build a workflow, use it as a weekly strategist. Every step has a screenshot of the real screen. The reader can follow in the app while swiping.

**The advice is in the contrast lines.** "Your Project Instructions shouldn't just say: 'write conversationally.'" and "Don't go straight from an idea to 'write me a Reel.'" Each names the common shortcut first, then gives the replacement. The last line, "Strategy → Approve → Create", is the whole post in three words.

**The CTA appears twice.** Step 1 carries "comment 'CLAUDE' if you want to set it up!" in the margin, and slide 8 repeats it with the promise "never run out of ideas and create content 10x faster". The early mention catches readers who stop after one step. Compare [[03 - I Built 9 Claude Skills]] for another post that shows skill files.
