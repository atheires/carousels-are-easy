## Notes

**The cover shows the whole org chart before it explains one box.** The seven numbered boxes are on the first slide, and the reader can count what they are being offered. "(I replaced all 7 with AI skills)" sits in brackets like a quiet aside, which is how the biggest claim in the post is delivered.

**The second slide is the only pain slide and it is one paragraph.** "You hit a wall and think the fix is 3 more hires... the agency walks off with all the IP. You are left with nothing and a smaller bank account." Then a six-word line on its own, "There is a leaner way." The relief is set up by the worst case.

**The org chart is reused, not redrawn.** Each role slide shows the same chart with only the active boxes lit and the sub-skills named in them. The reader keeps a map in mind and sees the system fill in. The text under it is a single paragraph, a job title and what it does, with a proof detail in the later roles ("Isabella ran this kind of system and saw a 69% revenue increase").

**The lines that carry are the short ones.** "Two full salaries, replaced by two skills, running off the same memo." and "Seven roles. Seven AI employees. Zero of them quit." They give the reader something to say about the post. Compare [[88 - The One Line That Makes Me $200,000 a Month]] for the same account's CTA logic.
