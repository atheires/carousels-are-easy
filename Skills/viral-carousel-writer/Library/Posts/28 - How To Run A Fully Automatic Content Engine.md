## Notes

**Slide 2 is a before/after diagram and it is the best-argued slide in the file.** Left side: one exhausted figure surrounded by five failing tasks, labelled `OVERLOADED. INCONSISTENT. BURNOUT.` Right side: six clean numbered boxes with one of them highlighted. The reader sees their current life and the proposed life in one glance, before reading a word.

**And the highlighted box is the concession that makes the whole thing credible.** Box 5 is `APPROVE — Taste gate`, with *"YOU DECIDE WHAT GOES LIVE"* written beside it. A post selling full automation deliberately keeps the human in the loop and says why: *"Without that gate, automation becomes spam. With it, you get consistency."*

**"AUTOMATE THE HEAVY LIFT. YOU KEEP THE TASTE."** Same division of labour as [[17 - How to Script Reels That Cross 1M Views|post 17]]'s workflow and as this vault's writer/author split — and the third time in this file that **naming the limit is what makes the claim believable**.

**"You are the bottleneck."** A three-word accusation as the first content slide, then four sentences that all start the same way — *"Ideas die… Research dies… Design dies… Posting dies…"* Anaphora used to make five separate frustrations feel like one condition.

**The metrics on screen are non-round and specific.** TREND SCORE 98/100, VELOCITY +312%, reaction scores of 92% / 78% / 24% / 18% / 12%. And the cutoff line has losers below it — showing what the system *rejects* proves it has judgement. **A filter is only credible if you show what it throws away.**

**"Claude is the creative brain; the image model is the hands."** One line that resolves a whole architecture. The kind of sentence a reader repeats to a colleague.

**Slide 7 is the slide almost nobody writes.** It documents the comment-to-DM mechanism *as part of the product*, including the auto-DM with `Complete-Workflow.pdf` attached and the `LEAD+ Added to Lead List` panel. **The post's own CTA is drawn inside the post as a feature.** The reader is shown exactly what will happen when they comment ENGINE — and then invited to comment ENGINE. Meta, and it removes all friction and suspicion at once.

*"This is the last mile most people skip."* — the line that turns a mechanical step into a competitive advantage.

**The CTA slide re-lists the deliverable as four numbered components with a benefit annotation on each.** Not "the workflow" — *trend scan + ranking rules*, *story + Claude prompt blocks*, *image + edit pipeline*, *Meta posting + Comment→DM map*. Same principle as [[03 - I Built 9 Claude Skills|post 03]] and [[09 - I Built My Second Brain With 3 Folders|post 09]]: **name the parts, and the whole feels real.**

**The comment word appears on slide 1 and slide 8.** *"COMMENT 'ENGINE' for the full workflow"* is on the cover, so anyone who bounces early still sees the ask. Only post in this file to front-load the CTA — worth testing, since it costs a little curiosity to buy a lot of coverage.

**Every slide carries the Claude and ChatGPT logos in the header.** Borrowed authority as furniture — the reader never has to be told the stack is real.
