## Notes

**Four words, one list or token row per slide.** The whole post is about 70 words. Each step has a bold word and an italic serif word ("Define Your *Foundations*"), a handful of bullets, and one small drawing of the thing. For a beginner topic the lightness is the point.

**The cover names the reader twice.** "Beginner Friendly Guide for UI Designers" and the title together say who it is for and what level. The red slide for Step 3 is the one break in the pattern and marks the centre of the process, components.

**The CTA is a full guide behind a topic word.** "Comment DOCUMENT and we will send you the entire guide", and "document your entire process" gives the reader a reason the guide is more than the four steps.
