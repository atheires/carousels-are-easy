## Notes

**The cover is a count and a time.** "6 carousels in under 1 hour" is easy to hold in the head and easy to doubt, so the reader swipes for the how. "using an ai system" is the only hint at the method.

**Slide 2 clears the ground in one line.** "Stop blaming AI for generic results. It's time to change how you use it." names the common complaint, assigns it to the reader's input, and the next slide starts the fix. It is the shortest argument slide in the post.

**Five steps, each opens with the thing to do.** Feed Claude your brand DNA, tell Claude who it is, analyse key information, use the 8-10 slide structure, don't ask Claude to write for you. The handwritten notes in the margin carry the reason ("This is how AI stops sounding like AI", "it writes like one").

**The good and bad prompt pair does the teaching.** Slide 8 puts one vague prompt beside one specific prompt with the reason written above each. The reader sees the difference instead of being told. The CTA, "a step by step how to set up personalized Claude", is the piece the examples cannot show. Compare [[75 - I Hit 10K Followers in 1 Day Using Claude]] and [[91 - My Claude Set Up That Saves Me 10 Hours a Week]] for the same space.
