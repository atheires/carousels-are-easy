## Notes

**The cover is a countable promise.** "You're only 100 LinkedIn posts away" turns a vague goal (become a thought leader) into a task with a number. Slide 2 repeats the phrase four times with a different prize each time, so the benefits arrive as a list with one rhythm.

**The objection is "this sounds crazy" and the answer is arithmetic.** Slide 3 gives two numbers (1 billion users, under 2% posting weekly) and one consequence (post once a week and you are in the top 2%). "And those billion people? They're the same people competing for your next job." turns the statistic into stakes.

**The real barrier is renamed.** "Cringe" is the author's word for what stops people. Slide 4 says it outright: "The cringe is the barrier. Not the action." Slide 5 draws it as a mountain with the reader on the slope. Naming the feeling lets the reader admit it, and it is the hook for the program, which "addresses cringe first".

**The sale is told as a personal circumstance.** "Buuuut here's the thing: I'm about to go on maternity leave." The reason for the offer is real and specific, so the price drop ($5,000 to $850) reads as a one-time event, not a discount. The "50 people" limit and "Once they're gone, they're gone" close it. Compare [[84 - How to Sell Anything to Anyone]] for the sales method the post follows.
