## Notes

**The headline is a list of eight, but every item is a confession.** *"We don't keep score because we both forget the score."* That is not advice — it is an admission of a shared deficit reframed as an accidental advantage. The whole post runs on that move: take the thing the audience is ashamed of, and show it working.

**Each item is a claim in the title and a personal cost in the body.** *"[1] We don't keep score…"* then *"In the past, people were always bringing up the things I did wrong 3 months ago… I felt at a severe disadvantage because I couldn't even remember 3 days ago."* The title is the payoff; the body is the wound it healed. **The reader has to feel the old pain to value the new state.**

**Nothing here is general.** *"We joke that we're 2 gremlins amongst humans."* / *"the same level of tolerance for disorganization."* No item would survive being rewritten for a general audience — which is precisely why they land. Compare with the failure mode in `Carousel Craft`: generic slides are what happens when the specific was never asked for.

**The staccato problem is absent because these are spoken sentences.** *"Literally everywhere we go, we're the ones having the MOST fun and joy."* Long, slightly run-on, capitalised for emphasis mid-sentence. It reads as someone talking, not as copy being emphasised — the distinction the vault has been trying to name.

**One photo, all nine slides, headline in the same position.** Identical to post 20's construction. This account's entire visual system is *one good photograph plus text*, and it costs nothing to produce. Worth noting against the assumption that carousels need design.

**The CTA asks for the reader's version, not for a keyword.** *"If you're in an ADHD-ADHD relationship I wanna hear your learnings too!! Drop it in the comments."* No deliverable and no capture — but unlike the weak *"comment if you want more"* CTAs in posts 14/15/17, this one gives the reader something to actually say. **An invitation to contribute beats an invitation to request.** It will produce a comment section full of stories, which is its own reach engine.

**Signed by both of them.** *"♡ Ari + Chirs"* — handwritten, at the end. It turns the post from content into a note.
