## Slide copy, verbatim

**1 — cover**

skills i'm keeping for myself vs. offloading to AI

*\* as an ambitious woman \**

*Collage of a woman at a laptop, a brain and a notebook, with two hands holding pens. Header on every slide:* THE ART OF UNLEARNING

**2**

SKILL I'M KEEPING FOR MYSELF:  
**writing content, scripts and captions**

I've tried making content with AI and it is horrific. It lacks **taste**, **character**, and **rawness** that comes from **real human experience.**

Sure, AI can write with your tone of voice, but it will never be actually you. It can understand a "formula" to structure it's writing to match how you write, but it always misses the mark slightly.

When you write your own content, script and captions, even if it's not "optimized", there is a **sprinkle of authenticity** that AI cannot replicate – and thats the **imperfectness** of you.

**3**

SKILL I'M KEEPING FOR MYSELF:  
**pen and paper for ideation**

I've tried brainstorming with AI for ideas for content and business and I started to realize 2 things:

1. The ideas weren't that great or generic
2. It made me feel dumber and lose my creativity

When I started going back to writing down my ideas, even if it was messy, all over the place, and made no sense, **I always had better ideas.** I could also feel my brain working harder as I was trying to go deeper on my own thoughts.

AI is good for general research, **but it is not creative or unique.**

**4**

SKILL I'M KEEPING FOR MYSELF:  
**responding to emails and DMs**

Many high performers will say this is a waste of time, but I disagree.

Whenever I get a DM from a follower or an email from a customer, I do not use AI.

**I want my personality to come through.** I want my customer to feel like "wow, this brand actually cares".

Or when a follower asks me a question, I want them to know it's actually me talking to them.

**Human connection over automation.**

**5**

SKILL I'M KEEPING FOR MYSELF:  
**deep learning via primary sources**

Alot of people use AI for research, and I think it's good for initial research, but it can also leave out **valuable sources.**

Now with **AEO** (agentic engine optimization), you can easily "hack" your way into being a source that AI references, even if it's not a credible source.

AI can give you general research about a topic, but often times it's just a summary about a topic.

If you truly want to go deep into a topic, I find using sources like PubMed, Google Scholar, physical books, even YouTube, is going to **help you learn better.**

**6**

SKILL I'M KEEPING FOR MYSELF:  
**discernment and decision making**

Never outsource your decision making to a robot, or to someone else.

Even if your decision isn't the "right one", at least you will have come to that decision yourself, and **you can learn from the decision.**

I used to always ask other people for their opinions, and it can cloud your judgement and **weaken your decision making skills.**

You need to learn how to trust your gut, backed with data, and then make a decision based on that.

You can get AI to help with drafting out pros and cons that you might not have considered, but **never let it decide the final choice.**

**7**

SKILL I'M OFFLOADING TO AI:  
**transcribing**

Instead of typing, I mostly transcribe via WisprFlow (which is technically an AI tool).

It does not alter anything I say, it simply transcribes word for word what I say into my online databases like Notion, Google Docs, Notes, emails, and even text messages.

I communicate best via speaking out loud. By using a transcription tool, it allows me to get my **most raw thoughts** down **without any editing or filtering** compared to when I write.

Even if it's messy, the sentences and tone of voice are **the most unique and creative.**

**8**

SKILL I'M OFFLOADING TO AI:  
**formatting documents**

After brainstorming my ideas and transferring it to my digital spaces, I find using AI for organizing information, formatting documents, and creating templates is extremely helpful.

This is a task I've never enjoyed doing and found as a waste of time. I know some people love making pretty templates, but not me.

This is a "skill" I'm willing to offload to AI to save time, because it can actually **do a better job than me**, do it faster, and takes away a task that I actually dread so I can focus on doing what I do best: **ideation and execution.**

This is admin busy work that AI should handle.

**9**

SKILL I'M OFFLOADING TO AI:  
**synthesizing information**

AI does a great job of summarizing information from books, scripts, documents, and publications.

However, I think it's important to **actually read** through these mediums on your own first, but it can be helpful to get AI to help you synthesize key learnings after you've done the work.

Think of this like a **second round of learning**, where AI can help remind you of the key points, or even pull out insights that you may have missed or forgotten about.

Whenever I've read a book vs. getting AI to just summarize the book, I learn and absorb the info from **first hand reading** way better.

**10**

SKILL I'M OFFLOADING TO AI:  
**reporting and analytics**

I will always use AI to pull data and analytics for social media, Shopify, Klaviyo, etc.

However, I will always review the raw numbers first, and then have **AI act as a second pair of eyes for what I might be missing.**

I don't want to lose the skill of **understanding how metrics and numbers work.** But I also want to make sure I'm not missing any key insights (which is where AI can help).

AI can also be helpful in helping me understand metrics, especially if I've never learned them before.

**11 — closing**

with AI increasingly dominating our world, way of life and work, it's extremely important to be a conscious AI user.

If you offload every skill and task to AI, you are no longer learning or using your brain. "Use it or lose it" is very real when it comes to cognitive function.
