**1 — cover**
**5 storytelling carousel formats going viral right now**

*Overhead photo of legs in grey knee socks and brown heels beside a leather bag. Words scattered across the frame at different sizes and angles rather than set as a block.*

**2 — 1. kinda chic**
*Three real @rebecca.therapy carousels: "kinda chic to not shrink yourself to fit in" · "kinda chic to start over in your 30s" · "kinda chic to do things solo" — 27.8K likes, 3,811 shares.*

Create a carousel using photos from your camera roll alongside short statements of things you think are "kinda chic." It's a simple way to share your personality, opinions, and identity while creating content your audience relates to and shares.

**3 — 2. split screen**
*Three real @sarah.oneiiil carousels: "doing the work is hard" / "carrying regrets is hard" · "believing in yourself is hard" / "doubting yourself is hard" · "growing is hard" / "staying the same is hard".*

Split each slide into two contrasting visuals or ideas to tell a story. This format is perfect for showing before vs. after, expectation vs. reality, old you vs. new you, or two perspectives. The contrast makes your message more memorable.

**4 — 3. things that are worth the money**
*Three real @teagan.maddux slides: "a film camera" · "things that are always worth the $$$" · "s'mores".*

Share products, experiences, habits, or investments you genuinely believe are worth paying for. The intrigue that builds with a list format, and the personal storytelling angle, is what makes this perform.

**5 — 4. reasons to follow or unfollow me**
*Three real @itsnataliecass slides: "my house is never perfectly clean" · "reasons to follow or unfollow me" · "I have ADHD and basically never shut up" — 16K likes.*

Flip the usual "follow me" message by giving people reasons not to follow you too. This new viral format communicates your values, attracts the right audience, and sparks engagement by making your content instantly stand out.

**6 — 5. re-introducing myself**
*Three real @angelmaybowyer slides: "Few Fun Facts" · "Hi, I'm Angel" · "What I'm into at the moment".*

A fresh take on the classic introduction post. Share who you are today, what you create, what you believe, and why people should follow your journey. It's an easy way to attract new followers while helping your existing audience feel more connected to you.

## Notes

**Every format is proven with three real carousels from a named creator, engagement numbers visible.** 27.8K likes, 16K likes, 3,811 shares. The post never claims these formats work — it shows five people they already worked for. **Borrowed proof at scale: fifteen screenshots, zero assertions.**

**Each format is named as a phrase the reader can copy, not a category.** *"kinda chic"*, *"reasons to follow or unfollow me"*, *"things that are worth the money"*. These are literally the words to put on slide 1. Compare a generic version — "opinion format", "contrast format", "listicle" — which would be true and useless. **A format named in the audience's own phrasing is a format they can execute today.**

**"Reasons to follow or unfollow me" is the standout.** Same inversion as [[20 - Hacks I'd Gatekeep If I Wanted ADHD Entrepreneurs To Fail|post 20]]'s gatekeeping hook: take the standard ask and reverse its polarity. The explanation is precise about why it works — *"attracts the right audience"* — which is the filtering job the vault assigns to the comment word, done on slide 1 instead.

**"Split screen" is a structural tool worth logging separately.** *Before vs after, expectation vs reality, old you vs new you, two perspectives.* The vault's craft file has no equivalent device — a slide that carries two things in tension rather than one thing stated.

**The cover breaks the account's own rules and gets away with it.** Words scattered at different sizes and angles across a photo, not set in a readable block — which [[16 - Your Carousel Is Dying On The Cover Slide|post 16]] would fail on the 0.5-second test. It survives because the phrase *"5 storytelling carousel formats going viral right now"* is scannable even broken up, and the aesthetic signals the audience (creators who care how things look).

**The CTA adds a stack, not a single file.** Same comment word as post 24 — *"story"* — but this time the deliverable is shown as a physical stack of three PDFs with a hand-drawn arrow reading *"+ Get 3 bonus PDF Guides"*, sitting on a laptop. **Same trick as [[03 - I Built 9 Claude Skills|post 03]]'s "(Plus 50+ others)": reveal that the thing is bigger than promised, one line before the ask.** One of the guides is visibly titled *"The Unscrollable First Post"* — a named artefact, which is what the weak CTAs in this file all lack.

**Two posts, one comment word, one offer.** Posts 24 and 25 both funnel to *"story"* and the same 21-day challenge from different angles. Worth noting against the vault's rule never to recycle a comment word across offers — recycling across *posts for the same offer* is correct and builds recognition.
