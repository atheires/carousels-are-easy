## Notes

**The cover leads with the money.** "I want to give you €400" is the whole offer in the first line, and "yes, you heard right..." answers the reader's disbelief before they voice it. The photos show what the money buys.

**This is an application post, not a lesson.** The slides run in the order an applicant needs: who it's for (2), what each side does (3), what the role is (4), what counts as an event (5), the one restriction (6), how to apply (7), the deadline (8), the ask (9). Nothing explains an idea; every slide answers the next practical question.

**The "you / me" split is a trade made visible.** Slide 3 lists what the reader does and what the author does in two short bullet lists. Her side is longer and more concrete, which is the proof the offer is real.

**The CTA is an email with a subject line.** No comment word. "Start subject line with 'Event Pitch'" is a filter, and "Send me an actual mini business plan, not just a quick message" raises the bar so only people who mean it apply. Compare [[35 - I Can't Keep This To Myself Anymore]] for another launch-style post with a plain ask.
