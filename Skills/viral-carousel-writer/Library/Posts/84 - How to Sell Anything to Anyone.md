## Slide copy, verbatim

**1 — cover**

How to sell  
*ANYTHING*  
to  
*ANYONE*

*Graph-paper background on every slide, words highlighted in lilac, green or underlined in coloured pen.*

**2**

1. Earn belief before the pitch

*they buy you before they buy anything else*

*WHAT:* people size you up before they size up your offer.  
*WHY:* trust removes risk. we say yes to who we believe.  
*HOW:* 1) give value first. 2) match their energy, don't perform.  
*EXAMPLE:* the one who helps even when there's nothing to gain.

**3**

2. Sell what changes, not what you're holding

*the object is just the delivery method*

*WHAT:* they're buying a better version of their life, not the item.  
*WHY:* we act on the picture we imagine, not the specs.  
*HOW:* 1) finish "so you can \_\_\_". 2) describe their after, in their words.  
*EXAMPLE:* not "a gym plan", "fitting your old jeans again."

**4**

3. Your ears close more deals than your mouth

*their words are the whole pitch*

*WHAT:* you can't fix what you never let them explain.  
*WHY:* people trust a fix built from their own words, not a script.  
*HOW:* 1) ask, then stay quiet. 2) repeat their problem back first.  
*EXAMPLE:* the doctor who asks before they prescribe.

**5**

4. Hand them the idea, not the verdict

*let it feel like their discovery*

*WHAT:* the strongest yes is the one they think they found alone.  
*WHY:* we defend our own conclusions, resist someone else's.  
*HOW:* 1) offer choices, not orders. 2) ask questions that lead there.  
*EXAMPLE:* "most people go with x, but it's your call."

**6**

5. Shrink the downside

what: the real objection is fear of getting it wrong.  
how: cut it with guarantees, trials, small steps, proof.

6. Ease outsells force

what: pressure breeds resistance, calm breeds trust.  
how: leave them an exit. it's what makes yes feel safe.

**7 — CTA**

You're not pushing a product. you're clearing the way for a yes.

earn belief.  
sell the after.  
listen first.  
hand them the idea.  
shrink the risk.  
ease off.  
master this  
and you can sell anything.

## Notes

**The cover is the biggest claim in the smallest space.** "How to sell anything to anyone", with the two big words in italic and highlighted. It promises a skill that applies to every reader, so the audience is as wide as it gets.

**Every principle gets the same four labels.** WHAT, WHY, HOW and EXAMPLE sit in four boxes around a hub on slides 2 to 5. The box shape lets a reader take one principle in a few seconds. Slide 6 drops the boxes and compresses two principles to "what / how" lines, which shows the post running out of ground to cover, not out of principles.

**The headings are mini-arguments.** "Your ears close more deals than your mouth", "Hand them the idea, not the verdict", "Ease outsells force". Each states the idea as a contrast, so the reader gets the point from the heading alone.

**The close turns six headings into one chant.** "earn belief. sell the after. listen first..." is a recap in one slide and the line that gets screenshotted. The ask is "Save this and Follow for more", no comment word. Compare [[26 - This Is How You Build An Irresistible Brand]] for a post that closes on a recap line.
