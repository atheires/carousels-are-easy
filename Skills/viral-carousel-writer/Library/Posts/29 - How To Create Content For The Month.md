## Notes

**The cover's two handwritten annotations carry the whole offer.** *"No Filming. No Scripting."* on the left, *"Using Ai Talking Heads"* on the right. Two objections killed and the method named, in six words, before the reader reads the headline. **Removing the two things the audience dreads is a stronger promise than the outcome itself.**

**"THE EASY AI TOOL STACK I STARTED WITH"** — *started with* is the load-bearing phrase. It signals beginner-safe and implies the author has since moved beyond it, which is confirmed on slide 6. The word *easy* is doing the NESB work.

**Every step names a real product and shows its interface.** Vocable.ai, Higgsfield with Nano Banana Pro, Muzecast.ai, ElevenLabs. Named tools with screenshots are the same proof mechanism as [[07 - You Need a Content Dashboard|post 07]]'s prompts and [[05 - How to Generate Ads With Claude Code|post 05]]'s Ad Library shot — **specificity that cannot be bluffed.**

**Step 2 is a four-item numbered micro-list inside a step.** The only slide that breaks into numbered sub-steps, because it is the only step that is a literal click sequence. **Format follows the content's actual shape** rather than being applied uniformly — the opposite decision to the inventory posts, and correct here.

**Each step closes on the time saved, not the feature.** *"before you touch a single other tool"* / *"without juggling five tools"* / *"One sitting, one afternoon."* The reader is being sold an afternoon, and every slide reminds them.

**Slide 6 gives away the ceiling of the method it just taught.** *"These three tools are the easiest way to start, exactly how I did it for months before I automated the whole thing through Claude."*

This is a sophisticated move: the post delivers a complete, working method — and then reveals it is the *beginner* version of what the author now does. The reader gets real value and simultaneously learns there is a further level. **Positioning the delivered method as a stepping stone creates the next want without withholding anything.**

**The comment word is "STACK"** — the exact noun from the cover slide, and it names the deliverable (*"my full tool list and guide"*). Consistent with every high-converting CTA in this file.

**Six slides for a four-step process.** No padding, no recap slide, no "let's get into it". The most economical Build post here — worth holding against the vault's 4–9 range as proof that the low end is viable when every slide is a step.
