## Slide copy, verbatim

**1 — cover**

any time I want to make a *stupid* amount of money, I open a blank note in my phone...

*Photo of the woman on a sofa with her phone. A blank Notes window sits over the bottom of the image.*

**2**

and this can apply to ANY BIG GOAL you have, btw...not just fitness.

**3**

i don't start with motivation and I definitely don't wait until i "feel ready."

*i open my notes app.*

(the first step will always be easy to do, but that's why not everyone will do it)

**4**

first, i write the exact goal. not "make more money." not "get healthier."

**the number. get TECHNICAL.**

i want to weigh 140 lbs. i want to run 3 miles. i want to make $100,000 in a month.

*Screenshot of a note:* THE GOAL: Make $100,000 in one month. Not "have a good month." Not "make more money." $100,000 collected revenue. I want to know EXACTLY when I've hit it.

**5**

you *don't* become a different person and THEN start acting differently.

you act differently long enough that eventually...

**you become a different person.**

**6**

same with fitness.

*not* ~~"lose weight."~~

more like...

*Screenshot of a note:* FITNESS GOAL: Lose 30 lbs. Get to 140 lbs. Fit comfortably into a size 4/6. Feel STRONG, not just smaller. Have good energy. Maintain it without spending the next 5 years losing and gaining the same 20–30 lbs.

**7**

and with money, i pick the EXACT number. (usually one that feels JUST out of reach.)

then i write what hitting that number would tangibly change.

*Screenshot of a note:* $100K MONTH. What would this actually change? More money invested in real estate. More money invested in my family. More money given away to ____. More help outsourced to buy time back. More incredible experiences with my family. More freedom to make decisions based on what I WANT instead of what something costs.

**8**

and i make those things measurable enough that **i can't lie to myself.**

*Screenshot of a note:* TODAY: Did I post? Did I make the offer? Did I follow up? Did I work out? Did I eat according to the goal? Did I keep the promises I made to myself? YES or NO.

**9**

then i reverse engineer the note.

if THAT is where i want to end up... what needs to happen this month? this week?

and eventually: what do i need to do *today?*

*Screenshot of a note:* OK SO HOW DO I ACTUALLY GET THERE? $100,000 this month = $25,000/week. What would need to happen every week? What needs to happen every day? What are the 3–5 actions that ACTUALLY create revenue? What offers am I selling and how many units? Everything else is secondary.

**10**

when i **quit drinking**, the note wasn't just "stop drinking."

i wrote what i wanted my actual life to feel like without it.

*Screenshot of a note:* WHY I'M QUITTING DRINKING: I want clear-headed Saturday mornings. I want to be fully present every single day. I want to stop waking up with that looming guilty feeling of "did I do anything wrong." I want to be able to feel uncomfortable emotions without numbing something to change how I feel. I want to get closer to God and be able to listen and hear His voice clearly. I want to feel FREE.

**11**

this is where giant goals get *REALLY boring.* which is great.

because i stop trying to "change my whole life" and just focus on the handful of things i need to repeat.

(this is also where most people give up, btw.)

*Screenshot of a note:* THE BORING PART. Do the daily stuff. Then do it again tomorrow. Then again. Stop needing every day to feel exciting. The giant goal is made up of a stupid amount of very normal days.

**12**

and if the plan isn't working, i change the HOW before i decide the goal is impossible.

change the route, keep the destination the same.

**there are always like 9,000 ways to get anywhere you're trying to go.**

## Notes

**The cover gives the method as a habit, not a promise.** "Any time I want to make a stupid amount of money, I open a blank note in my phone" sells a repeatable action the reader can do in the next minute, and the empty Notes window on the photo is the proof it is that small. The word "stupid" does the work a big number would do elsewhere.

**Every claim is followed by a screenshot of the note.** The slide says "get technical" and the next picture shows a goal written with a figure and a finish line. The reader never has to imagine what the instruction looks like. The same note frame repeats on six slides, so the repetition reads as a template they can copy.

**Slide 11 admits the boring part before the reader has to.** "This is where giant goals get REALLY boring. Which is great" plus "(this is also where most people give up, btw)" names the point the reader is most likely to quit, then turns it into the reason the method works.

**The CTA is a follow request, not a comment word.** It lists the author's four themes ("business identity, neuroscience, faith, money, habits") and ends on a threat of a missing feed. Compare [[113 - You're Looking for Content Ideas in the Wrong Place]] and [[88 - The One Line That Makes Me $200,000 a Month]], which both use a comment word instead.
