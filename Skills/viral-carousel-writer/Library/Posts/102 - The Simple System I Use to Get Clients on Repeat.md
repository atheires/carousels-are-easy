## Notes

**The cover is a promise of repetition.** "Clients on repeat (and how you can too)" sells a system, not a one-off win, and the parenthesis gives the reader permission to think it is for them. The first slide after it gives the proof: "$0 clients and no connections" to "$896K+" with four big brand names.

**The format is a quoted post, so every slide reads like a thread.** The white card, the avatar and the verified tick make each slide look like one tweet in a chain. The reader's habit of reading threads carries them through, and a single face behind every slide keeps the post a person talking.

**Each step is an instruction with a refusal in it.** "Make automotive work" / "Don't just make something cool and slap a logo on it" / "Don't post it and pray". The refusals name the three ways people fail at the same task, which makes the system feel earned.

**The method is shown as five verbs, then as a personal case.** Slide 9 is the portable version (Make the work → Make it for them → Make people care → Put it in their face → Repeat), slide 10 is the proof: "0 clients. $0 revenue. No connections. ... Eventually, the fake work became real work." The proof lands after the system, so it reads as evidence, not as the opening brag.

**The CTA is a waitlist with its contents listed.** "30-Day Fake Ad Challenge" and four bullets about picking brands, hooks, decision-makers and pricing. The bullets are the same steps the post just skimmed, so the reader knows what depth they would get. Compare [[89 - The ManyChat Strategy Behind a 7.4 Million Dollar Challenge]] for the other waitlist-style close.
