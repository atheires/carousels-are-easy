## Notes — this post is a critique of our own process

**Slide 2 is a diagnostic hook, and it is the best opener shape in the file for a professional audience.** It does not make a claim; it tells the reader to go and check their own data. *"Count how many got saved more than 50 times. Count how many drove DMs. Count how many people actually remembered a week later."* Three counts, escalating from a platform metric to a human one. Then the verdict: *"If the numbers are low, you've been treating Claude like a caption generator."*

**The reader convicts themselves.** They cannot argue with numbers they just looked up. Compare the [[13 - The Entire Industry Has Lost Its Mind|post 13]] hook — also an observation, but of someone else's feed. This one is of the reader's own.

### The four things it says to feed in before writing — all of which the vault already half-has

1. **Three past high-performing posts.** This is exactly `Successful Posts/DNA.md`. Confirmation the folder is the right idea.
2. **A list of banned AI words.** *Unlock, Tapestry, Leverage, Delve.* The vault has a kill list in the Headline Brain but no equivalent for slide copy. **This is a real gap.**
3. **A specific sentence-length rhythm.** Not "be conversational" — a measured rhythm. The vault says "write it as it would be said out loud", which is vaguer than this.
4. **A style guide, not an adjective.** *"We don't just say 'be funny.'"*

### The Hook Architect — a method the vault does not have

> *Identify the most common advice in the niche, then write 5 hooks that argue the opposite.*

This is a generative procedure for contrarian headlines, and it is more mechanical than the why-chain. The why-chain finds the *emotion* under a question; this finds the *received wisdom* to invert. They are complementary: the why-chain tells you what they are afraid of, this tells you what they have already been told a thousand times. **Together they are the gap sentence.**

Note it also asks for the audience's *"biggest hidden frustration"* — the thing they cannot say out loud, which the why-chain is designed to reach.

### Narrative arcing — three fixed temperature points

**Slide 1 High Tension → Slide 5 "Aha!" → Slide 9 Bridge to Action.**

Third independent confirmation of mid-post insight placement (see [[15 - Claude Carousel Blueprint|15]] and [[01 - The Neuroplasticity Theory|01]]). For a 4–9 slide carousel the equivalent is roughly: tension on 1, aha at the midpoint, bridge on the last.

**"Write like a screenwriter, not a marketer."** One instruction that would have prevented the copywriter-staccato failure already logged in `Carousel Craft`. A screenwriter writes what a person would say; a marketer writes fragments for emphasis.

### Visual friction — a genuinely new idea

> *"If the text is 'Simple,' the image should be 'Complex.'"*

The image should **contrast** with the text rather than illustrate it. Most of the weak posts in this file illustrate (post 14's swimwear photo illustrates nothing at all); the strong ones contrast — [[08 - Claude Sucks at Design|post 08]]'s furious voxel creature against a clean typographic claim, [[11 - Two Birthday Cakes|post 11]]'s delicate line-drawn cake against a story about childhood neglect.

### The Human Pass — a self-audit the vault should adopt

> *"What part of this sounds like a robot wrote it? Where am I being too formal?"*

And the advanced move: **rewrite the conclusion three times — as a Mentor, as a Peer, as a Skeptic — and pick the one that sounds most like you.** That is a concrete way to hand a voice decision back to the client instead of guessing it, which is exactly the writer/author split the vault is built on.

**Slide 8 closes on a question, not an instruction.** *"Are you using Claude to think, or just to type?"* It reframes the entire post as a standard rather than a workflow, and it is the line a reader would repeat.
