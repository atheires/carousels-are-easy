## Notes

**The cover is an insult with a condition.** "Your freelance web business is going to fail." is the prediction every reader wants to disprove, and "If you're doing sh\*t like this" makes it fair: only the ones who do the five things below. The opening line, "Web Designers & Developers:", calls out the audience before the hook.

**Every problem has the same build.** A small handwritten label ("Problem 1"), a one-sentence accusation as the heading, and two or three lines of plain explanation with a joke ("you don't need 10k followers though, dw", "then start doing good work lmao"). The jokes keep the tone friendly and take the sting out of the accusations.

**The fix slide splits the cause in two.** "All of these are business problems, not skill problems." The reader who has been defending their skill is told it is not the issue, which lets them read on without defending.

**The CTA is the answer to five problems with one free guide.** "I made a free guide explaining how to fix them. Comment STUDIO and I'll send you the full breakdown." The comment word is the author's studio name, and the post carries "Follow for more" as a second small button. Compare [[31 - Never Feel Guilty For Charging A Higher Price]] for the same audience.
