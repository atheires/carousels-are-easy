## Notes

**The cover joins two things that should not go together.** "My whole life is one claude dashboard" is a productivity claim; "my ai assistant is a cat" is a joke that makes the reader curious how a cat is the assistant. The second line gives the post personality and the first gives it a reason to be saved.

**Each slide has a number, a plain heading, a mockup and one italic line under it.** The italic lines are the post's quotable layer: "the 9pm mental sweep is gone.", "nothing lives in my head anymore.", "it's not a list. it anticipates.", "a notification you actually want to look at.", "if you can describe it, you can build it." Read alone they make a second, shorter post.

**The dashboard is shown before it is explained.** Slide 2 opens on the finished product, a morning screen with counts of what needs you, what is handled and what is drafted. The reader sees the benefit first and spends the rest of the post learning how.

**The recipe arrives at slide 6 and stays short.** Five numbered steps, each with the bold phrase to remember, ending "one afternoon. zero code written by me." The rule "draft everything, send nothing" is the one line a nervous reader needs to trust it.

**The CTA gives the build guide.** "comment dashboard and i'll send you the full claude build guide, prompts included", with the comment word taken from the cover's own wording. Compare [[07 - You Need a Content Dashboard]] for another dashboard post.
