## Notes

**The post is the method it teaches.** It uses one reel's transcript, picks six lines, and shows each as a slide with a reason underneath. The reader watches the reel become a carousel, which is more convincing than being told how.

**The rule is stated in two short lines.** "I don't rewrite the whole reel. I pull out the lines worth stopping for." Everything after it applies the rule. The word "worth" is the judgement the post is trying to pass on.

**Every example carries the reason in handwriting.** "Why it works: it makes the reader feel directly spoken to." Six different reasons (spoken to, simple to do, reason to react, more human, raises the stakes, line people save and send) turn the six slides into a small taxonomy of what a good line does.

**The lines are plain and sometimes blunt.** "Say the sh\*t others won't", "Be less formal", "Stop trying to be impressive." Each is an instruction in the second person with a one-line follow-up. There is no CTA slide and the post ends on its last example. Compare [[72 - Perfect Looks Fake Now]] and [[38 - Stop Sharing How Much Money You Make Online]] for the same account's other posts.
