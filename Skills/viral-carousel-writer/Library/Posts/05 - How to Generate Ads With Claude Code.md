## Notes

**The cover is the payoff, shown before the process.** The ad on the wall behind him on slide 1 is one of the four ads generated on slide 5. The finished result is the backdrop of the hook — you see the quality of the output before you are told anything about how it was made. Most process posts save the result for the end; this one leads with it and then explains it.

**Slides 4 → 5 are the single best move in the post.** Slide 4 shows the four ad concepts as *greyed-out empty tiles with only the headline visible*. Slide 5 shows the identical four tiles filled in as finished ads. Same layout, same positions, one slide apart. The reader's eye does the before/after by itself, with no "before" or "after" label anywhere. **A withheld version of the thing, then the thing.**

**Steps are numbered and each gets exactly one slide.** Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, Step 4. No slide carries two steps and no step spills across two slides. When the content genuinely is a sequence, numbering it removes all navigational effort from the reader.

**Each step is three lines: what it does, how it does it, what that gets you.** *"It studies your competitors. / Pulls every active ad they're running on Facebook right now. / Finds what's working, what's oversaturated, and where the gaps are."* Never more.

**Every claim has a screenshot under it.** The Meta Ad Library shot, the product shot, the finished ads. Nothing is asserted without something to look at — proof is visual rather than numeric here, which is the alternative to the claim-proof law when there are no numbers.

**The tweet frame is the texture device.** Every content slide is identical in layout — his avatar, name, tick, then plain text. As in post 03, an inventory or sequence tolerates total layout repetition. The frame also quietly borrows the authority of a verified account on every single slide.

**2,242 comments against 826 likes.** Nearly 3:1. The comment word "ADS" is short, on-topic, and the deliverable is named as a specific artefact — *the ads-manager skill inside AI Innovators* — not a vague "guide".
