## Notes

**The ask is a follow, not a comment word.** No lead capture, no withheld how. The entire value is handed over on the slides — the videos are named, linked visually, and described. Nothing is held back.

**It buys shares, not leads.** 569 shares against 32 comments — an 18:1 ratio. This is a save-and-send-to-a-friend post. Worth noting against the system's own CTA law: this post breaks "answer every why, hide every how" completely and still travels, because the *thing being shared is a resource list*, and a resource list with a gap in it is worthless.

**"(Part 8)" is doing real work.** It signals an archive behind the post, which is its own reason to follow — the follow is the CTA precisely because the series is the product.

**The top comment is an objection to the curation itself** — *"i don't know how fame or a youtube channel makes one a better artist"* (38 likes, 15 replies). The cover promised "better at art" and two of the six videos are about audience and monetisation. The gap between the promise and the contents is what the comment section argued about.
