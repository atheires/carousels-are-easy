## Notes

**The cover is blunt and is the post's voice.** "how to create ads that print (spoonfeeding you mfers)" and "P.S. please only use this for good. this is an incredibly powerful method." The swearing makes the post sound like a friend talking, and the P.S. makes the method sound more dangerous than it is, which is what makes a reader keep going.

**The deck has no images beyond two charts.** It is plain white lowercase serif on black, one thought of 20 to 50 words per slide. The argument has to hold the reader without design help, and it does through short separated lines and a plain build.

**The build is claim, borrowed authority, mechanism, example, method.** "you are selling the emotional outcome" (slide 2), the Hawkins map (3), "your job is to know where they are" (4), "move them up and then BACK down" (5), "emotional delta" (6), the poor-then-rich-then-poor story (7). The story slide makes a technical idea concrete before the process is named.

**The CTA hangs off the weak point of the argument.** Slide 8 says contrast cannot come from guesses, so research comes first. Slide 9 says AI can do the research, "but only if you feed it the right process", and the free thing is that process behind the comment word "FOUNDATION". The recap on slide 10 closes the loop with the cover's own phrase. Compare [[22 - Why Doing Everything Right Makes You Forgettable]] for a text-led argument with no illustration.
