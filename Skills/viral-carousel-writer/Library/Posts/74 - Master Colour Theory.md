## Notes

**The cover is a promise of mastery with no argument.** "Master colour theory" names the topic and the level in three words. The posts that work this way are references: the reader saves it, they do not need to be persuaded.

**One card, repeated, one wheel diagram per slide.** Every slide is the same layout: a name in capitals, one sentence on what it is, one clause in red on what it does ("high contrast and vibrant looks", "harmonious and calming effect"), and the wheel with that shape drawn on it. The red phrase is the only thing a skimmer needs.

**The first slide sets the vocabulary the others use.** Primary, secondary and tertiary come first, in a single three-row slide, because every later slide assumes the reader knows what a base colour is.

**The CTA is a plain follow.** No comment word, no freebie. "Save for later" on the last slide matches how the post is meant to be used. Compare [[10 - The 6 Golden Exercises]] for the same reference build.
