## Notes

**The cover is a claim in the form of a riddle.** "The one line that makes me $200,000/month:" ends on a colon, so the reader has to swipe to see the line. The only thing known is that it is one line.

**Slide 2 takes the reader's belief apart.** Everyone obsesses over the hook, gets 200,000 views and makes nothing: "Then rent is due and the views do not care." The sentence the cover was waiting for lands at the end: "The line that pays sits at the bottom of the post, not the top." The box diagram shows it too.

**The proof is the author's own post, shown.** Slide 3 gives views and leads in one sentence and two screenshots, one of the hook and one of the CTA, each with a handwritten note. The reader sees the same post doing both jobs.

**The funnel is told as a number sequence.** 200,000 views, 3,000 leads, a 9-day sequence, 30%, over $7 million in two years. Each step is one clause. "while I sleep" is the only line of persuasion.

**The post uses itself as the example.** The last slide asks the reader to comment "FOS", and slide 5 has just said the last line must be contextual to the content. The comment word relates to the free doc, and the doc is described in the post's own terms ("7 stages from stranger to client"). Compare [[40 - How To Make People Feel Something When They See You]] by the same account.
