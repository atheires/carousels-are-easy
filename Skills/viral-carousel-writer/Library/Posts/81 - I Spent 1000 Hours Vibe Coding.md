## Notes

**The cover claims hours, not results.** "I spent 1000 hours vibe coding (here's everything I learned)" sells depth through effort. The reader does not ask whether it works; they ask what 1000 hours teaches, and the thirteen numbered slides answer.

**Every slide has the same four-part card.** A numbered heading with the key word in orange, a handwritten aside that carries the one-line takeaway, a clay illustration of it, and a bordered box of three bullets (what, how, cost). Because the shape never changes, the reader can skim a slide in two seconds.

**The order is a ladder.** Slides 5, 6 and 7 are "Level One Plan Mode", "Level Two The .md Plan", "Level Three Superpowers", so the middle of the post is a progression the reader climbs. The slides before it say why planning matters and the slides after it cover what to do once the code exists (security, backup, readability).

**Fear is used sparingly and concretely.** "no rate limit = a 10k API bill" and "no RLS = your database is wide open" are the two lines a builder would repeat to a friend. The bullets give the exact word to ask Claude for ("ask for rate limiting by name"). Compare [[69 - How to Create Your 1st AI Automation]] for the how-to counterpart.

**The CTA delivers the video behind the carousel.** "Comment 'YouTube' and I will send you the full guide", with "22 MIN FULL GUIDE" and "100% free of course :)", states the length and the price, so the comment feels small.
