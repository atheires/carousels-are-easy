## Notes

**The post follows its own advice.** It is a design carousel about storytelling and every slide is one image with one sentence. Only the cover and the last slide have more than 20 words. The reader experiences the argument instead of reading it.

**The lines are built as pairs.** "Great visuals don't just look good. They speak." / "People don't remember 'Nice'. They remember what made them feel." / "Pretty doesn't make people stay. Emotion does." Each has a weak first half that the reader nods at and a stronger second half that carries the point.

**The practical slide is the shortest.** "Contrast is your drama. Whitespace is your pause. Scale is your emphasis." gives three tools as three metaphors, and "Together, they write your story without words." ties them. It is the only slide that could be read as advice.

**The ending is a question, not an offer.** "The only question is, What are you trying to tell?" and "Save for Later" on every slide are the whole ask. There is no comment word.
