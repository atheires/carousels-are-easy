## Notes

**The cover is three numbers in a row.** "5 rules. One image. 97,800 likes." The reader gets the count of what they will learn, the object it applies to and a proof number, and the sheep in the poppy field is itself an example of the rule being taught.

**The first body slide reframes the topic.** "Nobody teaches this: covers are the hard version of a hook. A reel hook gets sound, motion and autoplay. A cover gets a thumbnail and half a second." It takes a skill the reader may think they know (hooks) and gives them a harder, more specific version of it.

**Each rule has a number, a name, a tagline and an example.** The pattern is "ONE / THE WORLD / build the feeling, not the topic" with real covers beneath, so each rule can be seen at the size a cover is seen. One line in a box closes it ("Nobody stops for a topic. They stop for a feeling.").

**The post tells the reader its own method is not enough.** Slide 9 says the five rules are only the checklist and "watching one get built from nothing is the part that actually teaches it", which is the reason for the video behind the comment. The last slide asks for a follow as a condition ("MUST BE FOLLOWING to receive"). Compare [[16 - Your Carousel Is Dying On The Cover Slide]] for the same subject.
