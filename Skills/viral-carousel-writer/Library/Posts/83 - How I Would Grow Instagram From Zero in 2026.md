## Notes

**The cover promises a plan and the second slide takes it back.** "Here's how I would grow Instagram from zero in 2026. Just copy me →", then "I thought I had a plan... I was wrong." The post turns into a confession before any advice arrives, which is the reverse of what the cover led the reader to expect.

**The proof is two feeds side by side.** "This was my feed:" and "This is my feed now:" show the change without saying a number. The second grid carries real view counts (up to 23.6K) and a single creator, the first is a scatter of quotes. The reader sees consistency before being told it matters.

**The niche slide narrows by shrinking the type.** Twelve topics in grey stack down the slide, each line smaller than the last, then "Now my niche is: Storytelling." Seven words of copy do the whole argument visually.

**Three plain lines turn it into a rule.** "All your plans will change in 3 months. Guaranteed." then "Stop planning. Start posting." The advice is only two slides and it is the thing the reader came for, saved for after the evidence.

**The CTA is a joke the reader has to turn over.** "I never thought of it that way..." is printed upside down, which is the author's reaction. The ask underneath is a newsletter reached by commenting "email me". Compare [[22 - Why Doing Everything Right Makes You Forgettable]] for another post that argues from a brand's own shift.
