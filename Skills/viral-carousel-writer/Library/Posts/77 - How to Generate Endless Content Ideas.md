## Notes

**The first slide after the cover is a number and a gap.** "I create 247 pieces of content per month. Time spent? → 7 hours." The claim is too good to ignore, and the arrow leaves the reader wanting the how.

**Slide 3 names three objections and dismisses them with one line.** The three quoted excuses are the reader's own thoughts, then "That's not the problem." The cause is withheld until slide 4: "Content isn't about you. It's about them."

**The method is a week and a worksheet.** Slide 5 gives five days with five angles; slide 6 gives a fill-in list of 30 prompts. The reader can act on either without the author. "That's a month of content." closes the arithmetic the cover started.

**The story slide comes late and carries the belief.** Bali, one question, "What would help founders the most right now?" shows where the system came from, then the last slide reduces it to three parallel lines: systems, breakthroughs and rituals are content.

**There is no CTA slide.** The post ends on the reframe. Compare [[41 - How To Know What Content To Make]] for the same topic with a numbered path.
