## Notes

**The cover sets a budget as the constraint.** "If I only had $64/month" makes the list a decision, not a catalogue: the author had to choose. "The tools I use to save me 10-20 hours/week" is the benefit, and the four icons on the cover are the first four tools, so the reader sees the answer before they swipe.

**Each tool gets a price tag in the same place.** Name, then FREE or a monthly price in italics, then two sentences on what it does for the author. Seven of the first eight cost nothing or little, so the total can be checked by the reader. The jobs are given in the first person ("It runs my lead funnel without me touching it").

**The upgrade slide is a different shape on purpose.** Three tools on one slide with icons and prices turns the post from a list into a path, and the reader who is already done with the cheap stack has somewhere to go. It also shows the author knows the stack's limits.

**The CTA sells the part the list leaves out.** A list of tools does not say how they connect. "I'll be showing you exactly how to connect all of these into one system" names the missing piece and the comment word "WORKSHOP" matches. Compare [[90 - My Whole Life Is One Claude Dashboard]] for a similar tool post with a free guide instead of a paid event.
