## Slide copy, verbatim

**1**
The Neuroplasticity Theory
Why some people always seem calm.
@successoutright

**2**
Ever notice some people stay calm, even when things go wrong?

**It's not personality, It's not luck.**

**3**
Scientists discovered something wild about our brain.

It rewires itself based on what we repeat.

**It's called neuroplasticity.**

**4**
Try brushing your teeth with your other hand.

**It feels awkward.**

**5**
But do it for a few weeks.

Your brain adapts.
**It becomes automatic.**

**6**
And here's what most people miss:

**Thoughts are practice too.**

**7**
Every time you repeat negative self-talk

"I always mess up."
"This always happens to me."

**Your brain adapts.**

**8**
Over time when something goes wrong...

**Negative thoughts appear instantly.**

Almost automatic.
Because you trained them

**9**
If you interrupt negative thoughts and practice kinder reframes

**your brain starts learning a new path.**

**10**
Next time when a negative thought show up

Pause. Reframe.
**Choose a gentler default.**

**11**
Over time, you will notice
the kinder thoughts always show up first. Without effort.

**It becomes the new default.**

**12**
That's neuroplasticity in action.

You didn't change your life.
**You changed your defaults.**

That's how a happier brain is built.
