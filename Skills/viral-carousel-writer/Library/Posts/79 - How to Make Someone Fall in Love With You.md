## Notes

**The cover borrows a bigger topic than the post's.** "How to make someone fall in love with you using psychology" is a relationship hook. The slides are about content. The reader arrives for the first and stays because the next slide ("most people aren't attracted to you initially") is already about them.

**Each principle is named, then done to the reader.** Slide 5 says "your brain wants to see it resolved", and the line before it, "...& why you're still reading this", is itself an unfinished idea. The same happens with the layout: five principles with a slash-label ("02 / unresolved attachment"), one or two slides each.

**The final turn re-reads the whole post.** Slide 10: "every single one of these was being done to you the entire time. go back & read this again..." and slide 11 asks "what did you notice the second time?" The reader goes back through the slides, which doubles the time on the post, and the question is the comment prompt.

**One face, many photos.** Every slide has the creator in a different real setting. The text sits over the photo in plain white type, never boxed. The photos give the post personality while the claims carry the weight. Compare [[40 - How To Make People Feel Something When They See You]] for a similar psychology-led build.
