## Notes

**The framework is a three-word sentence.** Want, obstacle, change. The cover promises three ingredients and the next three slides name them in capitals, so the whole post can be remembered after one swipe.

**Each slide proves the ingredient with one sentence of the same story.** The three quotes read as one marathon story: "I wanted to run my first marathon." / "Three weeks before the race, I injured my knee." / "Six months later, I finally crossed the finish line." The reader sees a story built from the three slides without being told it is one.

**The photos do the explaining.** A runner from behind, the same runner on the ground, the runner at the line. Only about 15 words are on each slide, so the post is quick to read and easy to share.

**The CTA is not about storytelling at all.** "2000+ handpicked viral hooks" is a different free thing from the lesson, chosen for audience size. The comment word "Attention" is a topic word, not a word from the post. Compare [[50 - How To Become An Idea Machine]] for another very short framework post.
