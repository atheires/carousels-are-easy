## Notes

**The cover borrows the reader's rent.** "How an Instagram page can pay your rent" names the bill, not the income, and "in September" pins it to the month the reader is living in. The second line, "and how to start ASAP," turns the post into a plan.

**Slides 2 and 3 clear every objection with crosses.** Four red crosses (no dancing, no shipping, no begging, no trading time) and three more (no ads, no influencer, no desk) take the usual reasons not to try away from the reader before the story starts. The payout screenshot is the proof sitting between them.

**The story is a before-and-after told in the same two moves twice.** First the crosses and then "INSTEAD..." on slide 9 give the reader the life without the old job, then the sparkle list on slide 10 gives the life with the new one. "ALL BECAUSE..." hands the weight to the decision on slide 11.

**The CTA gives a year in the comment word.** "FREEDOM26" ties the offer to the year and the feeling, and the line "make sure to follow me so my message comes through" is the instruction the reader needs for the DM to land.
