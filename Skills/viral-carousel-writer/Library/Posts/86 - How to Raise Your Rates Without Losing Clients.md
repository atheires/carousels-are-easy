## Slide copy, verbatim

**1 — cover**

How to raise your rates ✱ Without losing clients

*Photo of a woman at a desk with two monitors.*

**2**

If you're stuck at ₹30k/month as a social media manager and you know you're worth more.

*Here's exactly how to do it:*

*Photo of two iced coffees.*

**3**

STEP 1: AUDIT YOUR CLIENTS

Divide your existing clients into 3 groups:

- **Dream clients** (pay well, easy to work with, results-driven)
- **Good clients** (decent, no drama, moderate pay)
- **Problem clients** (always asking for discounts, difficult, low pay)

**4**

STEP 3: DECIDE YOUR NEW RATE

What's your new rate?

Calculate:

- Your time spent per client
- Results you deliver
- Market rate for your experience
- What feels good AND sustainable

Don't jump ₹30k → ₹100k.  
Go ₹30k → ₹50k → ₹75k.

**5**

STEP 4: WHAT TO SAY TO EXISTING CLIENTS

Refer to this script (3 months before increase):

"Hey [name], I've loved working with you. Your account has grown [X%] and we've built solid systems together. Starting [date], my rates are increasing to ₹50k/month to reflect the value I'm delivering.

Your current rate stays ₹30k through [date] as a thank you for being an amazing client.  
After that, we can either continue at the new rate or I can help transition you to another manager.

Let me know your thoughts."

**6**

*Let me give you a reality check now:*

You might lose 1-2 problem clients. Good.

You'll gain new clients at higher rates. Better.

Your existing dream clients will stay. Best.

And your net result: Same number of clients. 50%+ more revenue.

You're not losing money. You're exchanging low-value clients for high-value ones. Your brain will fight this at first. And that's normal.

## Notes

**The cover names the fear in its second line.** "How to raise your rates / Without losing clients". The second half is the objection, so the reader who has been afraid to raise rates sees their own reason for staying stuck.

**The intro gives a real number and a reader.** "If you're stuck at ₹30k/month as a social media manager and you know you're worth more." The person and the figure are specific enough that the right reader says "that's me", and "Here's exactly how to do it:" promises a method.

**One step per slide, with the exact words to use.** Slide 5 is a full script with blanks ([name], [X%], [date]) and a number (₹50k). The reader can copy it, and the script itself carries the advice: give three months' notice, keep the current rate until then, offer an exit.

**The reality check meets the fear head on.** "You might lose 1-2 problem clients. Good. You'll gain new clients at higher rates. Better. Your existing dream clients will stay. Best." The good-better-best ladder answers the worry before the CTA. "Your brain will fight this at first. And that's normal." closes it.

**The free thing is a calculator for the number the reader just heard.** An income audit "to see how much you could potentially be earning" is the next problem after reading: what is my number?
