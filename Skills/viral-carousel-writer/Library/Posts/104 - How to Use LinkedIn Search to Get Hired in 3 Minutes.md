## Notes

**The cover gives a time limit that makes the method sound mechanical.** "In <3 minutes" tells the reader this is a shortcut with no research or writing, and "to get hired" names the result. The method is a search string, which is something a reader can copy and use at once.

**The whole post is six numbered instructions, one screenshot each.** Not a word of explanation sits beside any step. A reader who wants to do it can follow the screenshots alone, and a reader who wants to know why would need the author's other posts. The only reasoning is in step 6: "comment something thoughtful", which is the one step that needs a human.

**The copyable string is the value.** Slide 3 carries a Boolean search with the exact job title, the hiring phrases and the exclusions (intern, junior, entry level) in red italics. It is the part a reader would save. Compare [[41 - How To Know What Content To Make]] for another short procedural post.

**There is no CTA slide.** The final instruction is the ask. The author's name and photo on every slide, with a "Swipe >>>" cue, is the only branding. A later follow is the implied next step.
