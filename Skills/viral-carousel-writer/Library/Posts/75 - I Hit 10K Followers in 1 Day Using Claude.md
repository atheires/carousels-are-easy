## Notes

**The cover is a big number and a small admission.** "Hit it in 1 day using Claude" is the result, "I am no Claude expert, but here is exactly what I did" lowers the guard. The reader expects a hype post and gets told it will be a walkthrough.

**Every slide is a heading plus a short paragraph of what happened.** The headings run as a chain: "I started by giving it everything" → "Then we argued about my pillars" → "But every pillar had to tie back" → "Then I told it exactly what I wanted" → "Then it told me what was actually broken". Each one starts with "Then" or "But", so the reader follows a story in time order without a numbered list.

**The prompt is shown and withheld at once.** Slide 5 gives a shortened version and says the full one comes by DM on "claude". The reader sees the prompt is real, and the line that follows, "The role play wasn't the trick. Giving it a real number, a real deadline, and real data was", is the part they would have copied wrongly.

**The turn is a number against a benchmark.** "Only 0.13% of them followed me.... Healthy is somewhere between 0.4% and 0.8%." Slide 6 breaks the reader's assumption (it was not a reach problem) with the post's one precise figure, and slide 9 closes the loop with "0.13% to 0.83%".

**The close gives the real reason and a soft ask.** Slide 11 says none of it worked "because of just a prompt" and lists four human causes, which makes the earlier tool story honest. Slide 12 asks for a DM, then the comment word. Compare [[59 - This Connector Turns Claude Into a Content Machine]] for the same kind of tool post with the method shown.
