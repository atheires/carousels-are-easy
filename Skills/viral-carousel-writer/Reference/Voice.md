# Voice

Every carousel this skill writes sounds like the account owner. Their voice lives in the client's `Successful Posts/DNA.md` (built by the app's account study), with `About Client.md` and the `Feedback/` files on top. This skill does not carry its own voice; it reads that file every time.

## The steps, in order

1. **Read the client's `Successful Posts/DNA.md` in full**, and skim two or three of their own posts in `Successful Posts/`. Do this every carousel, before drafting any slide, so the first draft is already in their voice rather than corrected into it later.
2. **Draft the carousel** following `SKILL.md` here. The voice file governs the sentences; this skill's rules govern the slide shapes, the order and the ask.
3. **Voice pass.** After the draft passes the gate, read every slide aloud as the account owner and rewrite only the lines that fail: lines that sound like a presentation, lines that explain what a number already showed, lines with no real take.
4. **AI-tell scrub** over the whole text: remove signature AI vocabulary (unlock, leverage, delve, tapestry, game-changer), em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y" shapes, tidy punches of three and filler openers. Then check the voice markers from the DNA survived (at least three) and nothing was added that was not in the source.
5. **Gate.** The carousel then goes through the delivery gate in `SKILL.md`.

## Overrides: where a carousel differs from the prose a voice file describes

| Prose habit | In a carousel |
|---|---|
| Paragraphs are units of thought; no runs of one-line paragraphs | A slide is the unit. Within a slide, short standalone lines are allowed at earned moments (a pivot, a quote, the belief-shifting line). Across slides, shapes follow the format rules (`Formats and Slide Shapes.md`). No run of identical one-liners unless the set is parallel. |
| Headers only in structured pieces; never use a header to avoid a transition | Labels and short headings are normal on slides, and the transition lives in the last line of the slide before. |
| Bullets only for a real list of parallel items | Same here: 3 to 5 parallel verb-led bullets under 8 words, never a chain of reasoning. |
| Format fit beats length habits | Slide length follows the job of the slide (a pivot is 5 words, a reasoning slide is 60). |
| No "it's not X, it's Y"; no punch of three; no neat contrasting pair as a formula | Identical to R17 and R40 here. This rule wins over any swipe-file precedent. |
| Never invent | Identical to R1. Voice comes from how it is said, not from invented events. |
| Humour, parentheses irony, check-ins ("Right?", "You see?") | Allowed and wanted, one or two per carousel, never a quota (the dosage rule). |

## Quick voice check for slide copy

- Would they say this out loud to someone they know?
- Is there one sentence only they would have put here (a dry aside, a bent fragment, an irony in parentheses)?
- Does the emotion switch about every ten seconds of reading (a stat, then a dry line; warmth, then urgency)?
- Is the real take stated once, plainly, without repeating it?
- Are the contractions in?
- Is there a self-inclusion in the advice ("I'm telling this to myself too")?
- Is the vulnerability a reaction line, one or two sentences, never a performance?

## When the user corrects the voice

Ask nothing. Fix it, log it in this skill's `Feedback Log.md` if it is about slide craft, and add it to the client's `Feedback/` folder if it is about how that account writes.
